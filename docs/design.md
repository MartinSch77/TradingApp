Design element IDs (`DES-…`) are the constructive counterpart in the
traceability chain: requirement → design element → test. Each element names
the implementing classes/files and the requirements it satisfies
(`satisfies:` list is machine-read by `tools/trace_report.py`).

Architecture overview diagrams: see @ref architecture (PlantUML).

| DES-DOM-SWING | The trend-pullback strategy (2026-08-12 redesign, item 5): `ITradingStrategy` is the abstraction new trading ideas go through instead of another conditions chain folded into `BotSimRunner`, so several strategies can be run and scored SEPARATELY (`Prediction::strategyVersion`) rather than one growing chain whose pieces cannot be measured apart. `SwingPullbackStrategyV1` only trades WITH an established daily uptrend (close and EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 all stacked), on a controlled 2-5 session pullback that has come within one ATR of the fast EMA and today confirms the reversal (a higher low, or a close back above yesterday's high) — never a breakout chase and never a falling knife. | DES-DOM-PATHOUTCOME | Path-dependent decision labelling (2026-08-12 redesign, item 8): every RECORDED decision — including the ones that stayed out — should get a later label decided by the PATH price actually took, not an endpoint question ("was it higher after 180 minutes"). `resolvePathLabel` walks a hypothetical LONG and a hypothetical SHORT from the same entry price over the bars that followed; whichever reaches its own target (R-multiple, cost-buffered) before its own stop is the label; neither resolving in its own favour (including never resolving at all within the horizon) is `NoTrade` — "neither side covered its own costs and minimum move".
