# AI governance policy

This repository treats AI as a supporting tool, not as the final authority for safety, quality, or release decisions.

## Allowed AI use

AI may support:
- drafting feature and requirement text
- generating candidate design or test ideas
- summarizing evidence and process steps
- helping identify gaps or missing traceability links

## Not allowed as final authority

AI must not act as:
- the final release approver
- the independent QA sign-off
- the final safety decision maker
- the sole authority for requirement acceptance

## Mandatory human controls

Every AI-assisted item must have:
- a human reviewer
- a declared AI usage record
- a final human approval for acceptance
- explicit safety classification when applicable

## Safety-sensitive work

Any item classified as safety-relevant must be reviewed by a qualified human actor and must be signed off by a human final approver before release.

## Evidence rule

AI-generated output is accepted only as evidence when it is reviewed, classified, and recorded by a human in the project evidence package.
