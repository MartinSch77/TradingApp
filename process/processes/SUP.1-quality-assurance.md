# SUP.1 — Quality Assurance

See `process-model.md` Sections 2–3 before reading this file — they explain
*why* this process is not a rebrand of the existing verification tooling.

## Purpose

Provide **independent confirmation** that project processes and their work
products comply with the provisions defined for them (`processes/*.md`,
`work-products/*.md`) — and, where they do not, make that visible in a report
that the people who did the work cannot edit away.

## Inputs

- Every process specification in `processes/` (the reference QA audits
  against — never the current implementation's habits, per
  `process-model.md` §3.3).
- The repository's observable state: git history, `test-results/*.xml`,
  `analysis-results/`, `docs/traceability.html`, `CHANGELOG.md`,
  `process/traceability-matrix.md`, the risk register
  (`processes/MAN.5-risk-management.md`'s work product).
- `strategies/quality-assurance-strategy.md` for audit cadence and sampling
  rules.

## Outputs / Work Products

- `work-products/qa-report.md`-conformant report, published as
  `downloads/TradingApp-qa-report.md` (generated by `tools/qa_report.py`).
- Process-deviation entries filed into
  `processes/MAN.5-risk-management.md`'s risk register (QA raises the risk;
  QA does not resolve it — Section 5 below).

## Tasks

1. **BP1 — Develop the audit reference.** Confirm `processes/*.md` and
   `work-products/*.md` are internally consistent (`tools/check_process_docs.py`
   passes) before auditing against them — an inconsistent reference cannot be
   audited against meaningfully.
2. **BP2 — Confirm process conformance.** For each process in
   `process-model.md`'s landscape table, check that its defined tasks left
   the evidence its spec demands (a review record, a passing test run newer
   than the source it verifies, a tagged baseline, …). Recorded per-process
   as **CONFIRMED**, **NOT FOUND**, or **PARTIAL** (with the specific missing
   item named) — never a bare pass/fail with no evidence cited.
3. **BP3 — Confirm work product conformance.** Independently of process
   execution, check that each work product listed in
   `process/traceability-matrix.md` meets its own `work-products/*.md`
   content/quality rules (e.g. every `docs/design.md` entry names a
   `satisfies` requirement; every closed test run has 0 failures newer than
   its sources).
4. **BP4 — Report.** Publish `downloads/TradingApp-qa-report.md` per the
   cadence in `strategies/quality-assurance-strategy.md`. The report lists
   **every** process and work product from the landscape/matrix — an
   omission from the report is itself a QA process defect, not a silent
   "assumed fine."
5. **BP5 — Escalate deviations as risk.** Any NOT FOUND / PARTIAL finding is
   filed into the risk register the same day the report is published,
   addressed to Project Management and the process's own Accountable role
   (`roles.md`'s RACI) — QA's job ends at filing the risk; triage and
   remediation belong to MAN.5 and the audited process's own owner.

## Roles

QA (Responsible), Quality Manager (Accountable) — see `roles.md`. QA must not
be the Responsible or Accountable role of the process being audited for that
audit cycle (the independence rule, `process-model.md` §3.1).

## Base Practices (ASPICE 4.0 reference)

- **SUP.1.BP1** Develop project quality assurance strategy — realized by
  `strategies/quality-assurance-strategy.md`.
- **SUP.1.BP2** Assure quality of process execution — realized by Tasks 2/4
  above.
- **SUP.1.BP3** Assure quality of work products — realized by Task 3 above.
- **SUP.1.BP4** Ensure resolution and communicate details — realized by Task
  5 (escalation into MAN.5) and BP4's reporting requirement that a finding is
  never silently dropped.

## Verification / QA Hooks

This process audits itself through `tools/check_process_docs.py` (BP1) and is
in turn subject to `processes/PIM.3-process-improvement.md`'s effectiveness
review (`process-model.md` §6) — QA's own conformance is not exempt from
being checked, it is simply checked by Process Improvement's effectiveness
lens rather than by a second QA layer (avoiding infinite regress while still
closing the loop).
